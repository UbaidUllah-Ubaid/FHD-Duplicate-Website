Same Day CEREC Crowns at Ferny Hills Dental

At Ferny Hills Dental, we prioritise restoring your smile swiftly and seamlessly. As an experienced emergency dentist, we understand the importance of immediate care. Our Same Day CEREC Crowns provide a convenient, high-quality option for patients needing crowns without the need for multiple appointments. Thanks to advanced CEREC technology, we can design, create, and fit your custom crown in just one visit, helping you return to your routine with minimal disruption.
